I think the best way to stay ambitious is to daydream. A lot.

In every empty minute, my head goes to the same places: a robot you'd actually want to hug, a rocket I printed myself, a lab on the other side of the world. I replay them so often they stop feeling like fantasies and start feeling like plans. Eventually the thought gets so loud that doing nothing feels worse than starting.

That's how almost everything on this site began. Call it delusion; it's the only cure for procrastination I've found.

And when I don't know how to start? ELYT: everything can be learned from a YouTube tutorial.
