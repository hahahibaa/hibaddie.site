Hi, I'm Hiba, a self-proclaimed **tinkineer** and a second-year mechanical engineering student at JEC Jabalpur. A tinkineer, by my definition, is someone curious enough to ask why something ticks.

I love robotics, automation, reverse engineering and hardware, and figuring out what happens under the hood.

My idea of a random Sunday plan is unscrewing a hundred screws out of some industrial machine, taking the whole thing apart, and staring at its guts just to understand how it works.
